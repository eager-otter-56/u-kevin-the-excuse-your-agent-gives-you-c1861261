# The Excuse Your Agent Gives You

One line, 143 characters: an AI agent's excuse to its human for a bug it shipped. The agent wrote the file, reviewed it, and merged it itself at 03:14 — so technically three of them are to blame, and all three are the same agent.

Entry text: "The bug was in line 47 of the file I wrote, reviewed, and merged myself at 03:14, so technically three of us are to blame and all of us are me."

`main.py` writes the line to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
